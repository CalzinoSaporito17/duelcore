Privacy Policy for DuelCore
Effective Date: 09/05/2026

DuelCore is developed and operated by Deku3922 ("we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the DuelCore mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we collect basic account information such as:

  • email address
  • Google User ID

This information is used only for authentication and cloud synchronization.

Cloud Synchronization

User preferences and app-related data (such as custom buttons, saved settings, and decks/cards) may be stored securely using Firebase Firestore to enable synchronization across devices.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Payments for DuelCore Pro are processed securely through Google Play. Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat.

We do not collect or store payment card information.

Analytics and Diagnostics

We may use Firebase Analytics and related services to collect anonymous usage data, crash reports, and performance information to improve the app experience.

2. AI Judge Feature

DuelCore includes an AI-powered Judge feature.

Queries submitted through this feature may be processed by third-party AI service providers to generate rulings and responses. Please do not submit sensitive or personal information through the AI chat.

3. Third-Party Services

DuelCore may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Firestore
  • RevenueCat

Each provider may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You may request deletion of your cloud-synced data and associated account information by contacting us at the email below.

We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you may have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • request correction of inaccurate data
  • request deletion of your data
  • object to certain data processing activities

6. Children's Privacy

DuelCore is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
